I'm a **DevOps & DevSecOps Engineer** specializing in building **secure, scalable, and automated** cloud infrastructure. My expertise lies in bridging development and operations through modern platform engineering practices, integrating security at every stage, and leveraging AI to enhance operational efficiency.

```yaml

  - Platform Engineering & Internal Developer Platforms (IDPs)

  - Observability & SRE practices (SLIs, SLOs, SLAs)
```
